# Business Requirements Document (simulated)

## Problem
Leaders lack consistent visibility into SLA performance, backlog, cost, quality and capacity across Indian operations teams.

## Stakeholders (simulated)
Operations Director (accountable), Team Leads (owners), Finance Partner (cost), Quality Manager (control), BI Analyst (product owner), Technology BA (requirements/UAT).

## Requirements
FR-01 load linked operational records; FR-02 calculate reconciled SLA, turnaround, backlog, cost and quality KPIs; FR-03 drill from enterprise to city/team/service; FR-04 model capacity scenarios; FR-05 export reporting tables. NFR-01 deterministic reruns; NFR-02 validation failures are visible; NFR-03 documented assumptions.

## Acceptance
All KPI cards reconcile to the pipeline output, foreign keys are valid, scenario assumptions are labelled, and a clean run produces the dashboard and reports.
