# Evidence-backed findings

All findings below are observations from deterministic synthetic data; benefits are modelled opportunities, not realised corporate results.

1. **SLA exposure:** 74.6% of 497,839 completed requests met SLA; 126,451 breached.
2. **Backlog risk:** 2,161 requests remain open at the configured as-of date; 488 (22.6%) are already overdue against their SLA target.
3. **Complexity driver:** High-complexity completed work averages 60.6 hours versus 36.9 hours for low-complexity work.
4. **Cost exposure:** Recorded completed cost is ₹1,771,885,897; open work carries estimated remaining cost of ₹3,643,918 under the documented labour-rate assumption.
5. **Quality signal:** average quality is 91.24/100; the pipeline flags low-score quality incidents for exception review.
6. **Scenario:** a 15% turnaround reduction on Gurugram Payments Operations is modelled as a transparent opportunity, constrained to that observed workload and not presented as realised savings.
7. **Decision:** prioritise overdue backlog triage, complexity-aware staffing and quality controls; measure success with SLA compliance, overdue backlog, cost per completed case and quality score.
